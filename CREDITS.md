## The model and the weights

- **DeepSeek-AI:** [DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash) and its 1017 update (MIT), including the Engram tables, the vision encoder, and the DSpark draft layers that ship in the checkpoint (vLLM's `dspark` speculative method and the TensorFold engine run them). - **LibertAIDAI:** [DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-EXL3-3.5bpw](https://huggingface.co/LibertAIDAI/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-EXL3-3.5bpw) (MIT; also published as 2.9 bpw and NVFP4), the all-matrices EXL3 quantization of the 1017 update.
